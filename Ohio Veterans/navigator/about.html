About Us

What We Do

The Ohio Department of Veterans Services (ODVS) helps Ohio's veterans, service members, and their families get the benefits and support they've earned.

We train and oversee the 88 County Veterans Service Offices that work directly with veterans across the state, coordinate with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs on benefits, and operate two Ohio Veterans Homes in Sandusky and Georgetown. We also manage GI Bill approvals through the State Approving Agency, maintain more than 1.9 million veterans' service records dating back to World War II, and advocate for veterans at the state and federal level.

ODVS is a state agency, separate from the federal VA — so we don't make benefit decisions or give legal advice ourselves. That's exactly what your County Veterans Service Office is for, and it's exactly why we built the Navigator: to connect you with the right office and the right next step, without the runaround.
